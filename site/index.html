Play in 60 seconds

  1. Download RiftbreakSmash.exe from the Releases page.
  2. Double-click it. If Windows SmartScreen warns, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Pick a fighter, pick a stage, blast your rival off the screen. 4 stocks, 3 minutes.

Play with friends online

  1. Host: ONLINE → INTERNET ROOM → HOST ROOM — you get a 5-letter room code.
  2. Send the code to your friend. Friend: ONLINE → INTERNET ROOM → JOIN ROOM, types it in.
  3. Both pick fighters, host presses ENTER. No accounts, no port forwarding.

Same classroom? LAN play works too (the host PC needs port 7001 reachable). If school WiFi blocks everything, put both PCs on a phone hotspot.

How a match works

Damage %
Hits raise your percent — the higher it climbs, the farther you fly. There is no health bar.
Stocks & blast zones
Fly past the edge and you lose a stock. 4 stocks each, 3 minutes. Tied at timeout? Sudden death — both at 300%.
Drops & ultimates
Items rain onto the stage: weapons, shields, chaos pickups. Fighting charges your ultimate bar — every fighter has a screen-clearing finisher.
Win streaks
Keep winning and your fighter hits slightly harder, up to +20%. Lose and the bonus resets.

Modes

Solo vs CPU
Four difficulties from punching-bag Rookie up to Nightmare, which reads your habits. Learn a new fighter here first.
Internet rooms
Host gets a 5-letter code, friend joins from anywhere. Host simulates, guest mirrors — smooth on normal broadband.
Classroom LAN
Same WiFi, lowest latency. One PC hosts on port 7001, the other joins its IP. No internet needed at all.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no accounts, no microtransactions. One exe, full game.

Windows only? What about Mac / phones?

Right now: Windows 10/11 PCs only. It needs a keyboard (or an 8BitDo-style gamepad) and ~100MB of disk.

Windows says it "protected my PC". Is it a virus?

No — SmartScreen warns about any new unsigned program. Click More info → Run anyway. If you want, scan it with Windows Security first; it passes clean.

Online lags at my school. Fix?

Try an internet room first. If the school firewall eats it, use classroom LAN (host port 7001), or put both PCs on a phone hotspot — that almost always works.

Can I use a controller?

Yes — 8BitDo SN30 in XInput mode (boot with Start+X) plus most XInput pads. The game shows live pad status on the title screen.

Where are my wins saved?

On your own PC at %USERPROFILE%\.riftbreak\save.json — per-fighter records and your win streak live there, even offline.
