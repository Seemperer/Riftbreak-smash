RIFTBREAK SMASH

A free Smash-like 1v1 arena battler — 10 fighters, 18 stages, online rooms. No install, just run it.

⬇ Download for Windows

~17MB single .exe · Windows 10/11 · free forever

Play in 60 seconds

  1. Download RiftbreakSmash.exe from the Releases page.
  2. Double-click it. If Windows SmartScreen warns, click More info → Run anyway.
  3. Pick a fighter, pick a stage, blast your rival off the screen. 4 stocks, 3 minutes.

Play with friends online

  1. Host: ONLINE → INTERNET ROOM → HOST ROOM — you get a 5-letter room code.
  2. Send the code to your friend. Friend: ONLINE → INTERNET ROOM → JOIN ROOM, types it in.
  3. Both pick fighters, host presses ENTER. No accounts, no port forwarding.

Same classroom? LAN play works too (the host PC needs port 7001 reachable). If school WiFi blocks everything, put both PCs on a phone hotspot.

Controls (P1)

Move / jump
AD or arrows, Space / W ×2 to double-jump, Shift dash
Attacks
Z/J attack, X/K hold-charge smash, C/V specials
Super & ultimate
S/E super, F ultimate when the bar is full
Defense & extras
L shield, right-click SMART move, TAB move list, F11 fullscreen, 8BitDo SN30 supported

The roster & arenas

10 fighters with unique mechanics — triple-jump, meteor smashes, returning chakram, charge armor, reflect ×1.5, gravity wells, landing quakes, echo recall, lifesteal — across 18 scrolling arenas with lava, spikes, wind, ice, breakable ground and moving platforms.
